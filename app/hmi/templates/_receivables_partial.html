{# Receivables worklist — 2026-11-02 (Jessica). Money IN. "similar to expenses we can have pending and received payment options" The mirror of the expenses' "Payment Due" table, but for the money the CLIENT owes us. An invoice is PENDING until the money lands, then RECEIVED ("Received" == paid_at IS NOT NULL — one source of truth, no status-enum guessing, same as the expenses side). Context (from hmi.invoices): recv_outstanding — client invoices issued and not yet paid recv_drafts — invoices NOT yet issued (not money anyone owes us) recv_received — recently received (only when show_received=1) recv_include_received — bool, whether the received list is showing recv_today — date.today() recv_overdue_count — invoices past their due date and still unpaid recv_total_due — sum of the outstanding invoice totals recv_total_overdue — sum of the overdue invoice totals The bank-side provenance (method + reference) is recorded with the payment, so the pending_payment_reconciliations step (link a bank line to an invoice, then confirm) has something to match on. #}
