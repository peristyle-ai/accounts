Xero Export

Downloads a ZIP with three CSVs in Xero's standard import format — give it to your accountant and they can ingest it directly:

  • xero_invoices.csv — Sales / AR (Invoice template)
  • xero_bills.csv — Purchases / AP (Bill template)
  • xero_contacts.csv — Customers + distinct vendors (Contact template)

Option 1: All time

Exports every invoice, expense, and contact in the system. Use this when the accountant needs a clean baseline.

Option 2: Custom date range

Export only what's inside the range — useful for catching up on a specific period or handing over a partial year.

Notes for the accountant

  • Dates are DD/MM/YYYY (Xero's AU default)
  • Amounts are bare numbers (no $, no thousands separators)
  • Currency is AUD throughout
  • TaxType is mapped: 10% → GST on Income / GST on Expenses; 0% → GST Free …
  • Each CSV is UTF-8 with BOM — opens cleanly in Excel on Windows
  • Unknown columns (Status, AccountCategory) are ignored by Xero on import but useful for review
